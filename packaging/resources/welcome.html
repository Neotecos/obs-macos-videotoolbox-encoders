Apple VideoToolbox Customizable Encoders for OBS Studio

This installer adds two Apple VideoToolbox hardware encoders to OBS Studio:

  • Apple VT H264 Hardware Encoder (Customizable)
  • Apple VT HEVC Hardware Encoder (Customizable)

Tune their RealTime, encoding speed, and lookahead settings from Tools → macOS VideoToolbox Encoders…, and optionally let OBS use them automatically for Multitrack Video (Twitch, Amazon IVS, and Dolby OptiView).

The plugin is installed for the macOS user currently logged in. Restart OBS Studio after installation.

Requires macOS 13 or newer, Apple Silicon, and OBS Studio 31.1.0 or newer.
