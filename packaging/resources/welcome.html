Apple VideoToolbox RealTime Encoders for OBS Studio

This installer adds two hardware encoders to OBS Studio:

  • Apple VT H264 Hardware Encoder (RealTime)
  • Apple VT HEVC Hardware Encoder (RealTime)

The plugin is installed for the macOS user currently logged in. Restart OBS Studio after installation.

Requires macOS 13 or newer, Apple Silicon, and OBS Studio 32.2.2-compatible libobs.
